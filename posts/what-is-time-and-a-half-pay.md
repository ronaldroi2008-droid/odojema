Time and a half means an employee earns **1.5 times their regular hourly rate** for eligible overtime hours. This higher pay rate rewards employees for working beyond their normal schedule.

## Quick answer

Time and a half pay means earning 150% of your regular hourly wage for overtime hours worked.

**Formula:**

```
Time and a Half Rate = Hourly Rate × 1.5
```

**Example:**

- $15/hour → $22.50/hour
- $20/hour → $30/hour
- $25/hour → $37.50/hour

## What is time and a half pay?

Time and a half pay means an employee receives this for each eligible overtime hour:

```
Regular Hourly Rate × 1.5
```

Here is what that looks like at common hourly wages:

| Hourly Rate | Time and a Half Rate |
|---|---|
| $15 | $22.50 |
| $20 | $30.00 |
| $25 | $37.50 |
| $30 | $45.00 |

Time and a half is one of the most common forms of overtime compensation. ## How to calculate time and a half pay

Use this simple two-step formula:

```
Step 1: Time and a Half Rate = Hourly Rate × 1.5
Step 2: Overtime Pay = Time and a Half Rate × Overtime Hours
```

### Example: $20 an hour, 10 overtime hours

```
Step 1: $20 × 1.5 = $30
Step 2: $30 × 10 = $300
```

Total overtime earnings: **$300**.

To find your total pay for the week, add your regular earnings. If you worked 40 regular hours and 10 overtime hours at $20 an hour:

```
40 regular hours × $20      = $800
10 overtime hours × $30     = $300
Total weekly pay            = $1,100
```

## When do employees receive time and a half?

The rules vary depending on the country, employer, union agreement, and labor laws. Common situations include:

- Working beyond standard weekly hours
- Working beyond daily hour limits
- Working on holidays
- Working on rest days
- Special overtime arrangements

In the United States, federal law generally requires non-exempt employees to receive at least one and a half times their regular rate for hours worked beyond 40 in a workweek. Some states add their own rules, such as daily overtime. Employees should review their employment contract and local labor regulations to confirm eligibility.

## Time and a half vs. regular pay

Time and a half pays 50% more per hour than your regular rate:

| Hours Worked | Pay Rate |
|---|---|
| Regular hours | 100% |
| Overtime hours | 150% |

Using a $20 per hour wage:

```
Regular hour:             $20
Time and a half hour:     $30
Difference:               $10 more per hour
```

## Time and a half vs. double time

Many people confuse time and a half with double time. Double time pays twice your regular rate and is usually reserved for specific situations, such as long shifts or certain holidays, depending on the employer or local rules.

| Type | Multiplier |
|---|---|
| Regular pay | 1× |
| Time and a half | 1.5× |
| Double time | 2× |

Here is how they compare using a $20 per hour wage:

| Pay Type | Earnings Per Hour |
|---|---|
| Regular | $20 |
| Time and a half | $30 |
| Double time | $40 |

## Common mistakes when calculating time and a half

### Paying only the extra half

Time and a half means the whole overtime hour is paid at 1.5 times your rate, not just the extra 50%. At $20 an hour, the overtime rate is $30 per hour, not $10.

### Not starting with the regular hourly rate

Time and a half calculations should generally start with the regular hourly rate. Dividing your gross weekly pay by total hours worked can give you the wrong rate, especially if part of that pay is already overtime.

### Counting non-eligible hours

Not every extra hour automatically qualifies for overtime. Exempt employees, for example, are often not entitled to overtime pay. Always verify company policies and labor regulations.

## Frequently asked questions

**What does time and a half mean?**
It means earning 1.5 times your regular hourly wage for overtime hours worked.

**How much is time and a half for $20 an hour?**
Time and a half for $20 an hour is $30 per hour, because $20 × 1.5 = $30.

**How do you calculate time and a half?**
Multiply your regular hourly rate by 1.5, then multiply that rate by the number of overtime hours you worked.

**Is time and a half the same as overtime?**
Often, yes. Many employers use time and a half as the standard overtime rate, though specific rules vary.

**Is time and a half better than regular pay?**
Yes. Employees earn 50% more per overtime hour than their normal hourly rate.

**Is time and a half the same as double time?**
No. Double time pays 2 times your regular rate, while time and a half pays 1.5 times.
