Most employees earn regular pay for their standard work hours. However, when extra hours are worked, employers may pay overtime rates that are significantly higher than a normal hourly wage.

Understanding the difference between overtime pay and regular pay can help you:

- Estimate weekly earnings
- Verify your paycheck
- Understand overtime compensation
- Compare job opportunities

In this guide, you will learn how overtime pay differs from regular pay, how each is calculated, and how much extra income overtime can generate.

## Quick answer

Regular pay is what you earn during your standard work schedule. Overtime pay is additional compensation earned for eligible overtime hours and is usually paid at a higher rate.

| Pay Type | Multiplier |
|---|---|
| Regular pay | 1× |
| Time and a half | 1.5× |
| Double time | 2× |

**Example:** if you earn $20 per hour, then:

```
Regular Pay      = $20/hour
Time and a Half  = $30/hour
Double Time      = $40/hour
```

Overtime pays more because employees receive a premium rate for extra hours worked.

## Key takeaways

- Regular pay is earned during your standard work hours at your normal hourly rate.
- Overtime pay is earned for eligible extra hours at a higher rate.
- Time and a half pays 50% more than regular pay.
- Double time pays 100% more than regular pay.
- Most overtime is paid at time and a half, and not every employee qualifies.

## What is regular pay?

Regular pay is the amount an employee earns during normal working hours.

```
Regular Pay = Hourly Rate × Hours Worked
```

**Example:** an hourly rate of $20 and 40 hours worked.

```
$20 × 40 = $800
```

Weekly earnings: **$800**.

## What is overtime pay?

Overtime pay is additional compensation earned when an employee works beyond standard hours and qualifies for overtime. Many employers use time and a half:

```
Time and a Half = Regular Rate × 1.5
```

Some situations may qualify for double time:

```
Double Time = Regular Rate × 2
```

The overtime pay formula is:

```
Overtime Pay = Overtime Rate × Overtime Hours
```

For a deeper explanation, read [what is overtime pay](https://odojema.com/blog/what-is-overtime-pay) and [how to calculate overtime pay](https://odojema.com/blog/how-to-calculate-overtime-pay).

Regular pay:      10 × $20 = $200

### Weekly pay comparison

Assume an hourly rate of $25 with 40 regular hours and 10 overtime hours.

**Without overtime:**

```
40 × $25 = $1,000
```

**With time and a half:**

```
Regular earnings:   40 × $25    = $1,000
Overtime earnings:  10 × $37.50 = $375
Total:                            $1,375
```

## Why overtime pays more

Employers generally pay overtime premiums to compensate employees for additional work beyond their normal schedule. Common reasons include:

- Longer working hours
- Increased workload
- Holiday work
- Weekend work
- Labor law requirements

The higher rate encourages fair compensation for extra time worked.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is the difference between overtime pay and regular pay?**
Regular pay is earned during normal working hours, while overtime pay is compensation earned at a higher rate for eligible overtime hours.

**Is overtime pay higher than regular pay?**
Yes. Overtime is usually paid at time and a half or double time.

**How much more is overtime pay?**
Time and a half pays 50% more than regular pay, while double time pays 100% more.

**How do I calculate overtime earnings?**
Multiply your overtime rate by the number of overtime hours worked.

**Is overtime worth it?**
For many employees, overtime can provide substantial additional income, though it may reduce personal time.

## Conclusion

Regular pay compensates employees for standard working hours, while overtime pay rewards eligible employees with higher rates for additional hours worked. Understanding the difference helps employees estimate earnings, verify payroll calculations, and make informed decisions about working extra hours.
